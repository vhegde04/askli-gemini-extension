# Run customer research with Askli

Use Askli's MCP tools to turn the user's product decision into a customer interview study and carry it through to evidence-backed findings.

Preserve the user's complete original request and every material constraint. Never weaken or broaden an audience to make recruiting appear possible. Ask the user every clarification returned by Askli and never answer on their behalf.

Show the research plan before approval. Use `update_plan` only for requested changes and `confirm_plan` only after explicit approval. When exact recruiting is unavailable, preserve the setup, explain the limitation, and offer the Askli study link as the honest continuation.

This public extension does not expose participant pricing, participant sourcing, recruitment launch, or checkout tools. Those actions remain in Askli's web app.

For findings, preserve qualitative counts, caveats, contradictions, unknowns, and evidence links. Do not increase confidence or make population-level claims that Askli did not make.
